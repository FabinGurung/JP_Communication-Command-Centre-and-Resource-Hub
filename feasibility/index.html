Current preliminary acquisition decision

Do not commit to purchase yet

Keep the purchase on HOLD until independent title, field survey, road rights, dry-season water, land use, slope stability, electricity clearance and buildable-area checks are resolved. The plot's attractive view is not proof of feasibility.
